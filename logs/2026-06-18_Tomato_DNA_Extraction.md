# Tomato Leaf Genomic DNA Extraction, CTAB (2026-06-18)

**Date:** 2026-06-18 (precipitation overnight)
**Material:** 18 tomato leaf samples
**Method:** CTAB lysis with a handheld drill, two PCI extractions, isopropanol precipitation

---

## Preparation

* **Consumables per sample:** 2 × 1.5 mL tubes, 2 × 2.0 mL tubes.
* **Equipment:** dry bath pre-heated to 65 °C; motorized handheld drill.
* **Cleaning:** 70% ethanol wipes for the drill tip between samples.

---

## Reference protocol (CTAB)

1. Put the leaf samples into sterile 1.5 mL tubes.
2. Add 1 mL CTAB buffer and 5 µL β-mercaptoethanol (BME) and grind the tissue.
3. Incubate at 65 °C for 1 h (dry bath or water bath), inverting the tubes 10 times every 10 min.
4. Vortex.
5. Centrifuge at 13,000 rpm, 4 °C, 10 min. If the pellet and phases are not clearly separated, centrifuge for another 3 min.
6. Transfer 700 µL of supernatant to a new 2.0 mL tube.
7. Add 700 µL PCI (phenol:chloroform:isoamyl alcohol 25:24:1; 1:1).
8. Invert 10 times; centrifuge at 13,000 rpm, 4 °C, 10 min.
9. Transfer 500 µL of the aqueous phase to a new 2.0 mL tube.
10. Add 500 µL PCI.
11. Invert 10 times; centrifuge at 12,000 rpm, 4 °C, 10 min.
12. Transfer 450 µL of the aqueous phase to a new 1.5 mL tube.
13. Add 450 µL isopropanol (1:1) and mix by inversion.
14. Incubate at −20 °C overnight.

## Bench record and deviations

1. **Tubes**
   * 36 × 1.5 mL tubes (initial collection and final precipitation) and 36 × 2.0 mL tubes (first and second PCI extraction) for the 18 samples.

2. **Lysis buffer and grinding (deviation)**
   * 18 leaf samples; soil and debris were removed from the leaves.
   * Only 300 µL CTAB buffer and 5 µL BME were added before grinding (reference: 1 mL), so that the drill would not splash or overflow the tube.
   * The tissue was ground in the tube with the motorized drill until fully disrupted.
   * The remaining 700 µL CTAB buffer was added after grinding (1 mL in total).
   * The drill tip was wiped with ethanol between samples to avoid carry-over.

3. **Lysis at 65 °C**
   * Tubes were inverted after the buffer addition to mix buffer and tissue.
   * 65 °C for 1 h in the dry bath; tubes were inverted for 10 s every 10 min.

4. **First centrifugation**
   * All 18 samples were vortexed and centrifuged at 13,000 rpm, 4 °C, 10 min.
   * The phase boundary and pellet were not clearly defined, so the samples were centrifuged for another 3 min under the same conditions (allowed in reference step 5).

5. **Supernatant**
   * 700 µL of supernatant was transferred to the first set of 2.0 mL tubes without disturbing the pellet.

6. **First PCI extraction (deviation)**
   * 700 µL PCI (1:1); inverted for 10 s.
   * Centrifuged at 12,000 rpm, 4 °C, 10 min (reference step 8: 13,000 rpm).

7. **Second PCI extraction**
   * 500 µL of the upper aqueous phase was transferred to the second set of 2.0 mL tubes.
   * 500 µL PCI was added.

8. **Second PCI centrifugation (deviation)**
   * Inverted for 10 s; centrifuged at 13,000 rpm, 4 °C, 10 min (reference step 11: 12,000 rpm).

9. **Isopropanol precipitation**
   * 450 µL of the aqueous phase was transferred to the second set of 1.5 mL tubes.
   * 450 µL isopropanol was added (1:1) and the tubes were inverted several times.

10. **Overnight precipitation**
    * All 18 tubes were placed at −20 °C overnight.
