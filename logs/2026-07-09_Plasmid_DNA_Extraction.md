# Plasmid Extraction: pGEX-5X-1 from *E. coli* DH5α (2026-07-09)

**Date:** 2026-07-09
**Material:** overnight culture of *Escherichia coli* DH5α carrying pGEX-5X-1
**Method:** alkaline lysis and silica spin columns (FAPD buffers), two preparations of 2.8 mL culture each
**Result:** 123.5 and 125.8 ng/µL in 40 µL (NanoDrop)

---

## Culture (before this log)

* The DH5α::pGEX-5X-1 glycerol stock is taken from −70 °C without letting it thaw.
* It is streaked on LB agar with the selective antibiotic and incubated overnight at 37 °C to obtain single colonies.
* A single colony is inoculated into 2.0–5.0 mL LB with the antibiotic in a 14 mL round-bottom tube with a loose or vented cap, and grown overnight at 37 °C with shaking.
* The cells are pelleted by centrifugation for the extraction (steps 4–5 below).

---

## Reference protocol (FAPD kit)

| Step | Buffer | Volume | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| Resuspension | FAPD1 (with RNase A) | 200 µL | resuspend the pellet |
| Lysis | FAPD2 | 200 µL | alkaline lysis (do not vortex) |
| Neutralization | FAPD3 | 300 µL | precipitate genomic DNA and proteins |
| Wash 1 | WP | 400 µL | column wash |
| Wash 2 | Wash buffer (with ethanol) | 700 µL | column wash |
| Elution | elution buffer or ddH2O | 50–100 µL | elute plasmid DNA |

1. Pellet 1.0–3.0 mL of culture at 11,000 × g for 1 min; remove all supernatant.
2. Add 200 µL FAPD1 (RNase A already added) and resuspend completely by pipetting; no clumps should remain.
3. Add 200 µL FAPD2 and mix by gently inverting 5–10 times; RT for 2–5 min, not longer than 5 min. Do not vortex (it shears genomic DNA, which then co-purifies).
4. Add 300 µL FAPD3 and invert 5–10 times immediately.
5. Centrifuge at maximum speed (~18,000 × g) for 5 min. Place a FAPD column in a collection tube.
6. Load the supernatant onto the column without the white pellet; 11,000 × g, 30 s; discard the flow-through.
7. Add 400 µL WP buffer; 11,000 × g, 30 s; discard the flow-through.
8. Add 700 µL Wash buffer (96–100% ethanol added); 11,000 × g, 30 s; discard the flow-through.
9. Dry spin of the empty column at ~18,000 × g for 3 min. Residual ethanol inhibits downstream enzymes and lowers elution efficiency.
10. Place the column in a clean 1.5 mL tube.
11. Add 50–100 µL elution buffer or sterile ddH2O to the centre of the membrane; RT, 1 min. Volumes below 50 µL lower the yield.
12. Centrifuge at ~18,000 × g for 1 min; store the plasmid at −20 °C.

## Bench record and deviations

1. **Bench**
   * Bench, pipettes and equipment wiped with 70% ethanol.

2. **Tubes**
   * Four sterile 1.5 mL tubes: two for pelleting and lysis, two for elution.

3. **Culture**
   * The Falcon tube with the *E. coli* DH5α culture was placed on ice.

4. **Pelleting, first round (deviation)**
   * 1.4 mL of culture (2 × 700 µL) into each of the two tubes.
   * Centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 3 min at RT (reference: 11,000 × g, 1 min).
   * Supernatant removed without disturbing the pellets.

5. **Pelleting, second round (deviation)**
   * Another 1.4 mL of culture was added to each tube on top of the first pellet, centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 3 min and the supernatant removed.
   * In total, 2.8 mL of culture was pelleted per tube.

6. **Removing residual medium**
   * After blotting the inverted tubes, the remaining medium on the walls and around the pellet was removed with a pipette.

7. **Resuspension**
   * FAPD1 (with RNase A) was taken from −20 °C; 200 µL was added to each tube and the bottle was returned to −20 °C straight away.
   * The pellets were resuspended with a 200 µL pipette until the suspension was uniform and no clumps remained.

8. **Lysis (deviation)**
   * 200 µL FAPD2 per tube.
   * The tubes were inverted more than 15 times (reference: 5–10) to mix the buffer through the dense suspension, gently to avoid shearing genomic DNA.
   * RT for 5 min.

9. **Neutralization (deviation)**
   * 300 µL FAPD3 per tube; inverted more than 15 times (reference: 5–10).

10. **Clearing spin (deviation)**
    * Centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 5 min at RT (reference: ~18,000 × g, 5 min).
    * Two FAPD columns were placed in a rack.

11. **Column loading (deviation)**
    * 600 µL of cleared supernatant per column, taken with a 1000 µL pipette without touching the white pellet.
    * Centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 5 min at RT (reference: 11,000 × g, 30 s).

12. **WP wash (deviation)**
    * Flow-through discarded; 400 µL WP buffer per column.
    * Centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 1 min at RT (reference: 11,000 × g, 30 s).

13. **Ethanol wash (deviation)**
    * Flow-through discarded; 700 µL Wash buffer (with ethanol) per column.
    * Centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 1 min at RT (reference: 11,000 × g, 30 s).

14. **Dry spin (deviation)**
    * Flow-through discarded; empty columns centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 3 min at RT (reference: ~18,000 × g, 3 min).

15. **Elution tubes**
    * The columns were moved into the two clean 1.5 mL tubes from step 2.

16. **Elution (deviation)**
    * 40.0 µL elution buffer onto the centre of each membrane (reference: 50–100 µL), to obtain a higher concentration.

17. **Elution spin (deviation)**
    * Centrifuged at 12,000 rpm for 1 min at RT (reference: ~18,000 × g).

18. **Concentration (NanoDrop)**
    * 123.5 ng/µL and 125.8 ng/µL for the two preparations.

19. **Storage**
    * Tubes labeled "26.07.09. pGEX-5X-1" and stored at −20 °C.

## Deviations from the reference protocol

| Step | Reference | This run |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Culture volume | 1.0–3.0 mL | 2.8 mL, pelleted in two rounds |
| Centrifugation | 11,000 × g or ~18,000 × g | 12,000 rpm for all spins |
| Pelleting | 1 min | 3 min per round |
| Column loading spin | 30 s | 5 min |
| Wash spins | 30 s | 1 min |
| Inversions (lysis, neutralization) | 5–10 | more than 15 |
| Elution volume | 50–100 µL | 40 µL |

The 1 min standing time before the elution spin (reference step 11) is not recorded.
