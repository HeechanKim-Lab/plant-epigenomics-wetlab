# Half-Strength MS Medium (2026-07-01)

**Date:** 2026-07-01
**Batch:** 1 L of 1/2 MS medium with 0.7% plant agar, autoclaved as two 500 mL flasks

---

## Reference protocol

| Component | Amount per 1 L | Role |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| Murashige & Skoog (MS) medium | 2.15 g | half-strength basal medium |
| Sucrose | 10 g | carbon source |
| MES | 0.5 g | buffer |
| Plant agar | 7 g | gelling agent |
| pH | 5.8 | — |

1. Dissolve 2.15 g MS medium, 10 g sucrose and 0.5 g MES in distilled water (DW) to a volume close to 1 L.
2. Adjust the pH to 5.8 with 1 M KOH or 1 M HCl.
3. Bring the volume to 1 L with DW.
4. Split the medium into two containers of 500 mL.
5. Add 3.5 g plant agar to each container (7 g per litre).
6. Autoclave at 121 °C for 15 min.

---

## Bench record

1. **Glassware**
   * One 1 L beaker, one 1 L and one 500 mL graduated cylinder, and two 1 L Erlenmeyer flasks (for autoclaving and storage), all rinsed with DW.

2. **Dissolving the components**
   * About 800 mL DW in the beaker on a magnetic stirrer.
   * Balance tared with weighing paper; spoon wiped with 70% ethanol.
   * MS medium (2.15 g), sucrose (10 g) and MES (0.5 g) were weighed one after the other and added to the stirring water.

3. **Agar**
   * Two portions of 3.5 g plant agar were weighed and put directly into the two Erlenmeyer flasks.
   * Flasks were labeled and closed with aluminium foil.

4. **pH**
   * pH meter calibrated with pH 4.0 and pH 7.0 standards.
   * pH adjusted to 5.8 by adding 1 M KOH dropwise while stirring.

5. **Volume and splitting**
   * The solution was transferred to the 1 L graduated cylinder and brought to 1 L with DW.
   * 500 mL was measured with the 500 mL cylinder into each flask containing the agar.

6. **Autoclaving**
   * Both foil-covered flasks were autoclaved on the liquid cycle at 121 °C for 15 min.
   * After depressurization, the flasks were moved to a clean bench to cool for pouring.
